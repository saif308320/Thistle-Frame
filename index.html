The Photographer

Isla Ferguson

I started Thistle & Frame in a spare room in Leith, with one camera and a conviction that wedding photography should feel less like a performance and more like a memory being kept honestly.

Twelve years and several hundred weddings later, that conviction hasn't changed — only the light I know how to find, and the quiet moments I know how to wait for.

Trained in editorial portraiture before turning to weddings, I bring a documentary eye to every ceremony and a painter's patience to every portrait.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book?+

Most couples book 9–14 months ahead, especially for peak season (May–September). Popular Saturdays in Edinburgh can go a year or more in advance.

Do you travel outside Edinburgh?+

Yes — we regularly shoot across the Lothians, the Borders, and the Highlands. Travel within 25 miles of Edinburgh is included; further afield is quoted separately.

How long until we receive our gallery?+

Wedding galleries are delivered within 6–8 weeks. Corporate events are typically turned around within 5 working days.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Absolutely. A 25% deposit secures your date, with the balance split into interest-free instalments due before the event.
